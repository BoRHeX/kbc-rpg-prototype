# Cataclysm 2.0 release blockers after Pass 10

| Blocker | Evidence in 1.9 | Evidence required for 2.0 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Two independently firing hand weapons | The production hero draws and aims a tail mount and combat uses the existing tail shot path. The Trial borrows that build. The checkpoint has the current equipment and upgrade state. [Combat](../game.js) · [Checkpoint](../checkpoint.js) | Show two additional visible, independently equipped, upgraded and auto-aimed hand mounts. Verify each muzzle, cooldown, pierce, bounce and save round-trip under movement-only input with three mounts firing and bounded projectile work. |
| Connected underground biomes | Trial encounters cycle through flooded street, stair landing and sealed door. The runtime parks the campaign and uses dedicated trial walls and arena coordinates. A clear saves a badge and restores the surface run. [Trial director](../threshold-trial.js) · [Runtime](../game.js) | Demonstrate persistent descent, exploration and recovery across the city boundary. Validate route and wall clearance, discovery, checkpoints, encounter rewards, underground fishing and hazards, and authored biome art and audio. |
| Sphinx finale | The Trial culminates in the Mourning Bell Sovereign. The Long Night ending and Endless unlock rely on the separate Night 1000 story clear. [Trial director](../threshold-trial.js) · [Campaign](../long-night.js) | Author and playtest the Sphinx sequence, with readable phase tells, decisions that exercise squad and build choices, deterministic phase and reward restore, and one-time ending credit. |
| Device and endurance evidence | The scripted Trial cleared one seeded solo build, and a second build exercised both fusion choices and timed out. The campaign suite advances in segments and restores chapter checkpoints. Local Chrome aborted before page load. The WAV audit estimates decoded sizes from file headers. [Campaign log](pass10-campaign-final.log) · [Chrome log](pass10-chrome.log) · [Media log](pass10-media.log) | Capture working Chrome touch and pixels in both orientations and a 15–30 s Trial recording. Run both an instrumented 30-minute session and a complete campaign on representative physical Samsung hardware, including background/resume with weather and audio active. Measure frame times, retained audio, active objects, and late-game difficulty. |

Pass 10 leaves the 2.0 production decision with the creator.
