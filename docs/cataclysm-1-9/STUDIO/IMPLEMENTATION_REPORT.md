# Cataclysm 1.9 · Pass 09 implementation report

Pass 09 integrates the inherited 1.8 game, the Pass 08 media and the Threshold Trial. Scope follows the [owner-authorized ten-pass order](../../../game_factory/CATACLYSM_17_19_TEN_PASS_ORDER.md), the [approved roadmap](../../../game_factory/CATACLYSM_1_1_TO_2_0_ROADMAP.md), the [studio policy](../../../game_factory/STUDIO_MULTIPASS_PRODUCTION_POLICY.md) and the [Pass 08 concept](CONCEPT.md). This is an integration handoff with passing deterministic fixtures and a blocked Chromium launch.

## Playing 1.9

- **Title and pause.** Resume shows the saved chapter and night. Save & return to title checkpoints before leaving. Starting a new campaign over a resumable one asks for replacement confirmation. The same settings controls appear at title and pause, with volume, mute, fullscreen where supported, larger panel text, compact HUD and reduced motion. How to play covers movement, tail auto-fire, autonomous company and optional encounters. The removed controls belonged to manual squad tactics, companion abilities and runic activation. [HTML](../index.html) · [Responsive styles](../game.css) · [Runtime](../game.js)
- **Records.** World Map opens at title, pause and results and masks undiscovered districts and routes. Bestiary records on-screen sightings and confirmed defeats, and reveals mutant counterplay and guardian identities. Company shows the 16 evolutions and three working fusions. Mecha forms automatically. Trawl Team and Spotter Pair form through the pause controls. The gallery uses matching inherited fusion frames and painted section illustrations. Fishing shows bait odds, active bait and charges, resolved outcomes including Lantern Koi and Jackpot Cache, and actual jackpot deltas. [Runtime](../game.js) · [Company](../company.js) · [Fusion rules](../advancement.js)
- **Threshold Trial.** Reach campaign Night 101 and open Trial from an eligible saved run or a safe paused campaign. Reuse or change the seed, take the current squad, choose available pair merges for the attempt, or enter solo. Clear tagged enemies and stand in the beacon for two continuous seconds in each of three 40-second legs. Rain slows dashes across the flooded street, fog narrows acquisition on the stair landing, and storm warnings mark strikes that stun eligible enemies at the sealed door. Adds can fight and reanimate during the attempt. Only tagged deaths unlock the beacon. A clear saves the Threshold badge and best time. [Director](../threshold-trial.js) · [Combat adapter](../game.js)
- **Trial isolation.** The adapter clones the build, gives the attempt full HP and one life, and parks the campaign and combat RNG. It uses the standard collision, projectiles, auto-fire and companion systems in a bounded arena with a 12-enemy cap. Pause and hidden documents stop the fixed-step clock. Clear, defeat, timeout and abandon restore the parked run. Reload recovers the campaign save. Badge time lives under a separate storage key. [Runtime](../game.js) · [Checkpoint adapter](../checkpoint.js)

## Data, media and pacing

The 1.9 A/B journal uses schema 3, a separate writer lock, checksums and revision checks. It imports supported 1.8 and 1.7 slots read-only, with earlier city metadata handled by the inherited importer. History initializes empty on migration, even when earlier saves have catch or jackpot totals. Cast and award rows each retain the latest 64 entries. Company and bestiary retain the highest observed state. [Journal](../long-night.js) · [Validation](../checkpoint.js)

Pass 08 supplied all three generated originals, the derived painted cover and icons, three scene crops and six PCM WAV cues. The title uses the responsive cover sizes. Record pages use section icons, and each trial leg draws its arena crop underneath the combat geometry. Menu and trial cues stop on pause, mute or background and use the current volume. The combat score and enemy audio reuse the inherited runtime. [Generated originals](IMAGE_MANIFEST.json) · [Derived assets and WAVs](POLISH_MEDIA.json) · [Media generator](build-polish-media.py)

Normal spawns require a free offscreen point. Scripted encounters use collision-tested locations and warning time where they enter near play. Enemy and companion routing share padded obstacle waypoints and a short escape step at corners. Late chapters increase the threat budget and shorten healthy-player spawn intervals up to 24% within the existing interval floor and live caps. Pass 09 keeps the inherited boss HP formulas.
