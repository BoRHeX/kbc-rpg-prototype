ORIGINAL MOBILE ARCADE

Orbit Jump

An original mobile browser game. Open and play.

Creative decision · 3 pitches

Selected: Orbit Jump · The player controls an avatar moving at constant speed around a fixed circular orbit around the screen's center, using one-finger drag to slowly adjust orbit speed within limits. Targets periodically appear along the orbit. The player taps once to make the avatar jump inward along the radius to collect a target before quickly returning to the orbit's perimeter. Missing a target or colliding with a moving enemy on the orbit ends the round.

Why: Orbit Jump balances fresh, fun gameplay with simple one-finger controls, ensuring immediate player engagement and feasible implementation by addressing manageable UI risks.

Rejected alternatives:

  • Lumen Weave: High originality but risk of unclear goals and steep learning curve that could reduce fun and accessibility on first play.
  • Magnet Maze: Innovative mechanic complicated by dual input demands and potential mobile performance issues, risking smoothness and player comprehension.
