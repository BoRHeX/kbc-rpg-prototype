The alley survival guide

“My paws do the running. Yours do the steering.” — BoRHeZ

01 · Keep movingDrag anywhere in the arena, or use WASD / arrows. Lift to stop. Your cat aims and fires automatically.

02 · Follow the fishCollect fish and XP to grow. Three fish within eight seconds trigger a Fishbone Encore when ready.

03 · Build your luckLevel up, then choose from three cards; perks unlock four or five. Combat freezes. Spend earned rerolls here to refresh the choices.

04 · Trust your crewRecruit up to four distinct companions. All 16 roles eat their own fish and earn individual Levels 1–3. Level-3 Engineer + Shooter automatically form one Mecha slot; heat forces repairs. Work 24 / 75 earns Levels 2 / 3; each eaten fish adds 6 work. HUD: work/threshold, f = fish eaten, E/S = Engineer/Shooter Level-3 readiness. Crew components stay committed until restart. Other fusion pairs remain unknown and are not unlockable. Stand still near your healer for care.

05 · Respect the big catsBosses hit hard. Watch their warnings, keep an escape lane and avoid getting surrounded. Runes and squad tactics activate automatically.

06 · Nine-life warrantyZero HP consumes one life. Revive after 0.9s at 60% HP with 2.8s protection; XP, fish, squad and night remain. Zero lives ends the run. A rare eligible Terminus guardian can restore one life up to nine, once per city seed. The old timed life lottery is disabled.

07 · Take a breatherPause with Ⅱ or Escape. Use Tab to reach controls; arrows select upgrade cards, Enter chooses. Your sound level is remembered.
