# Cataclysm 1.6 · Mutations After Midnight
## Pass 01 · Encounter and production specification

After midnight the city learns the cat's habits. A bait-shaped creature waits on the shortest route, a bell borrows bodies from the next alley, and a thief watches the fishing apron. Give the player time to read intent and choose a route with the same movement control that drives the white quadruped and its automatic tail gun.

## Encounter rules

Use simulation time and a seeded encounter stream independent of fishing and city RNG. Resolve damage/death and eligible interrupts before an action commits on the same simulation step. Each special activation has a unique token. Publish cues on state entry and clear pending tokens, markers and membership on death, retreat or reset. Pause freezes gameplay states and telegraphs. Preserve live state across ordinary life loss, cancel pending harmful actions into recovery and re-arm only with a full warning after inherited grace. Draw damaging footprints for the whole warning. If the warning cannot be seen in the playable viewport, defer or cancel the action. Lock targets before impact and permit one inherited damage transaction per activation. New enemies deal zero contact damage outside explicitly armed states.

Use `safeSpawn`, `nearestSafeSpace`, terrain clearance, open-district checks and connectivity to the player's walkable component. The city's physical bounds, solid buildings, gates and water remain authoritative. Validate body footprint and swept path at commit and during movement. If revalidation fails, stop into recovery. No nearest-point teleport into damage. Roof entry is painted motion above the city with the physical actor admitted only at a validated landing socket. Keep existing wing/phase terrain behavior and exterior/locked-district clamps. Sample a bounded eight sockets per admission, then defer.

Charge new actors against inherited global population and threat limits as well as these provisional limits. Wave means campaign encounter ordinal, with admission counters reset only on the campaign's wave transition. Ordinary director ticks never reset them.

| Resource | Live cap | Wave cap |
|---|---:|---:|
| New ordinary mutants and their descendants | 8 | 12 admissions |
| Bell-Ringers and fusion forms carrying a bell | 1 combined | 2 combined admissions |
| Bell recruits | 2 | 2 total |
| Necro-Groomer / reanimated bodies | 1 / 2 | 2 / 2 |
| Pack Leader / commanded followers | 1 / 3 | 1 / 3 newly admitted followers |
| Roof Pouncer / Mimic Stray / Thief | 2 / 2 / 1 | 3 / 3 / 2 |
| New fusion or cocoon | 1 | 1 attempt |
| Evolving elite | 1 | 1 admission |
| New damaging windups | 1 before Night 80, 2 thereafter | 3s shared start spacing at one lane/choke |

Allocate at most 14 new threat units per wave plus at most 6 reserved for the elite. Mimic, thief and each recruit cost 1, roof and leader 2, bell and groomer 3, each revival 1 and fusion offspring 4. Existing followers can be reassigned without respawning; they remain charged to global population/threat. Consume wave capacity on successful admission, and reserve it at windup start for jobs that spawn. Release the reservation on cancellation and keep the spent attempt token. Fusion swaps parents for one cocoon and then one offspring, with ancestry and budget checked at both boundaries. Global pressure, recovery, low life, active gate/guardian/fishing danger, and major encounters take precedence. Suspend new encounter admission during these reserved beats. On blocked navigation or prolonged distance, disarm then retreat after 8s and release live slots without kill credit. All creation goes through bounded admission, including revival and summons. Keep gate/campaign credit categories exclusive. At Night 80+ choose varied flanks and exposed recovery routes. Record pressure, warning exposure, hits and recovery gaps for solo tail versus strong squad, rather than scaling HP to match squad DPS.

## Six state graphs

Durations are simulation seconds. 'Hit' below means confirmed positive damage from player or company, including Mecha. Recovery allows the tail to finish a target while the player moves. No new input binding.

- **Mimic Stray · The Window Dresser.** A ragged cat folds its fins around a single counterfeit sardine, horseshoe magnet or scrap pickup. Enter through a 0.7s uncurl/fold tell, then disguise for up to 12s. Disguise → reveal (within 85 world units or hit, 0.95s) → locked straight lunge (0.25s) → exposed recovery (1.5s) → stalk or retreat. Snap twice and show a split-tail outline in reveal; movement perpendicular to the painted lane evades. Hit during reveal delays entry into lunge by replacing it with recovery. Disguise uses the enemy actor only, with a visible breathing seam, never a real pickup record. Keep 120 units clear of genuine pickups, fishing aprons and beacon routes on admission. Limit to one lunge per life of the disguise, no re-disguise. Defeat yields one genuine fish once through `addFish`.
- **Bell-Ringer · The Last Conductor.** Bent eartips and a bronze pendulum wrapped in an old ribbon. Arrive with a muted bell lift (0.8s) → seek reachable visible shoulder of the street → raise bell (1.35s, expanding dashed circles and two dry taps) → ring once → recover (2s) → ordinary pursuit. Any hit during raise cancels and consumes the ring attempt. The ring causes zero damage and admits up to two weak recruits at clear sockets with 0.85s arrival grace, using reserved budget. Recruits cannot summon, combine or provide corpses. Chase the raised paw with automatic fire or take the open side of the street.
- **Roof Pouncer · Gutter Acrobat.** Flat ears, long rain-slick limbs and a gutter-hook tail. Select reachable point from 0.35s projected player motion → show widening striped shadow and downward chevrons for 1.35s, locking target after 0.35s → descend (0.24s, no en-route damage) → resolve one landing footprint → crouched recovery (1.7s) → hunt on ground or retreat. A visible roof-edge pose is targetable during the warning when its body socket is legal. Hit there cancels into grounded recovery if reachable, otherwise retreat. After first landing it gets one grounded repeat after 6s, with full warning. Sidestep after lock. Reject any socket without a reachable escape outside the footprint before impact.
- **Necro-Groomer · The Undertaker.** Wide apron of stitched fur, hooked comb and a habit of smoothing wrecks. Arrive with comb held upright (0.8s) → seek eligible wreck (within 140 units) → reserve one corpse and channel (1.6s, visible dotted braid) → consume and revive → cooldown (8s) → seek or stalk. Hit cancels braid and unlocks corpse with a 2s reservation lockout; death releases it immediately under that lockout. Each groomer gets at most two channel starts. Record only ordinary original zombie deaths, maximum six live markers, 12s expiry, one consumption per ID. Revived bodies rise with 1s harmless stagger, carry no corpse, reward or fusion eligibility and expire after 12s. A blocked revival socket cancels and releases reservation. Following the braid lets the player target its caster before the rise.
- **Pack Leader · The Crossing Guard.** A broad torn traffic vest and forked tail flag signal orders. Enter with raised chevron aura (0.85s) → claim up to three weak unclaimed pack cats in reachable streets → stage frontal and side lanes (1s, chevrons and flank stripes) → lock formation (0.5s) → sequential charges (0.25s each, gap 0.35s) → regroup (2.5s). Reserve all attack paths, leave an open lane, and cancel any single charge whose path becomes invalid. Followers stay harmless while staging. A hit during lock cancels the next charge and sends the leader into regroup. Leader death immediately cancels all queued charges, staggers followers harmlessly for 1.2s and removes aura before ordinary pursuit resumes. Followers cannot be reclaimed that wave. Let the formation commit, then take the open side, or break the captain with focused fire.
- **Feral Fish Thief · The Pantry Runner.** Wiry legs, hook-shaped nose and a torn creel with a white flap. Arrive with sniff-and-bolt tell (0.8s) → seek a reachable ordinary ground-fish ID or investigate an active cast's bait scent → race to target → visible paw hover (0.85s) → attempt atomic theft → chew (1.4s) → flee (max 5s). Search twice per second, with an 180-unit radius, no claw damage. Lose a target on pickup or pausing the transaction through damage: any hit during hover cancels into 1.2s recovery and leaves the fish. Without an eligible target, paw at the edge of the reserved apron for 1s when attracted by a baited cast, then retreat. The decoy consumes neither bait nor a catch and cannot trigger cast cancellation by contact. One successful theft per thief, up to two per wave. Cut across its route, claim the fish during the hover, or break off fishing to intercept.

## Finite fusion and evolving boss

**Charnel Carillon.** After Night 45 a live bell and groomer with unused special actions may pair once per wave, only in one reachable neighborhood within 110 units and outside reserved encounters. Suspend their individual actions, then use inherited cocoon cadence as a model in `beginFusion` and its update path. A 1.8s spiral of comb teeth and bell ribs marks the pair-to-cocoon transition. Hit either parent before commit to cancel, release reservations and send both into recovery. Commit consumes both once, replacing them with a destructible cocoon for 1.4s. Destroy it to cancel emergence and pay one fish once. On emergence a single fused body rings two alternating hollow lanes. Warn the first for 1.4s and the second for 1.2s after 1.0s recovery. Each briefly animates one corpse inside its own lane under the shared revival cap, decaying after 8s. Without an eligible corpse the lane remains harmless and the cast is spent. Hit during a lane warning cancels that lane. Cross behind the cleared lane or break the braid. After two casts, or 18s from emergence, retreat. No ancestry is eligible for further fusion or revival. Test the existing wing/phase attacks and native cocoon emergence/destruction separately.

**The Midnight Bailiff.** Offer one optional evolving elite from Night 80 when a guardian has already been provoked this run and no active guardian, gate, fishing danger or scheduled major is present. Snapshot current open district and provoked guardian IDs from city records. Never invoke guardian rewards, gate credits or final-wave bookkeeping from the elite. Spawn with 1.3s harmless silhouette reveal. Choose phase-one lanes from that district's reachable street axes, using the most recently provoked guardian's district as a deterministic orientation preference. Phase-two safe sectors use the same snapshot. Reserve both escape routes before admitting the elite. Preserve ordinary DPS and XP mechanics.

1. **Toll Collector.** Lantern-jawed four-legged inspector. Telegraph two parallel sweeps with striped lines and a double knock for 1.45s, lock after 0.4s, sweep 0.3s, recover exposed 2s. The open corridor and space behind the shoulder remain traversable. Confirmed hit in recovery advances stagger toward canceling its next sweep, with three hits within one recovery as provisional threshold. At 55% HP, defer transition until the current danger clears. Also transition after three sweeps to exercise phase two in solo builds.
2. **Broken Procession.** Split mantle and raised lantern tail. Change pose with 1.25s harmless molt and clear previous markers. Mark opposite sectors with hollow broken arcs for 1.55s, lock after 0.35s, close for 0.3s then grant 2.4s exposed recovery. Two sectors remain safe and one reachable must be visible. A hit during the first 0.35s interrupts the close once per elite. Sweep and close never overlap each other or another new attack. At 45s total the elite disarms and exits without kill credit. Defeat yields a fixed four physical fish once plus ordinary kill XP, with no jackpot or extra life. Solo tail can shoot while crossing safe streets. Heavy squads can shorten exposure through damage, with no phase healing or mandatory invulnerability period.

## Physical fish and company

In `game.js`, keep `fishingTick`, `fishingResolve`, `fishingHazard`, reserved outcome/ordinal, charged bait debit, rest cooldown, dwell and jackpot IDs authoritative. Angler autonomous work and Courier redistribution keep their own existing paths. Bait scent is a read-only event. Give `addFish` items stable run-local IDs and source/eligibility metadata. Eligible theft targets are genuine ordinary combat drops and resolved ordinary catches, one whole item of at most two units. Protect legendary catches, guardian/cache/major rewards and any task-owned fish. A thief has no access to inventory, a pending catch, or companion food. Late-game test changes to food scarcity independently of ordinary drop rate.

Make player pickup, `COMPANY.eat` and theft resolve through one synchronous ground-item removal, once per ID. Resolve same-step claims in player → companion iteration order → thief order. Split ledger into spawned, player, eaten, ground, held and lost so spawned = player + eaten + ground + held + lost. Keep `caught` as provenance. Ground→held happens before any XP or food bookkeeping for the thief, which has neither. On chew completion held→lost. If defeated before chew completes refund floor(units/2), moving that amount held→ground with a new physical ID and no new spawned credit. Remaining held units become lost. Defeat on the same tick as chewing wins. If the refund position is invalid choose a nearby reachable socket, or classify the units as lost if none exists. On retreat/reset settle held exactly once before archiving/discarding the run ledger. Use zero defaults for old counters. Preserve kill XP and add pickup XP only on actual player collection. Test Angler's own fish accrual, Courier's transfer and Mecha's automatic hit interrupt without changing Mecha slots, build, board, fire, heat or repair.
