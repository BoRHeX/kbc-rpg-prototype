# Cataclysm 1.69 · THE WILD CARD

Pass 01 design for the continuing Cataclysm game. The production base is the published PR #96 1.6 source. Release order is 1.6 → 1.69 → 1.7 THE LONG NIGHT → 1.8 → 1.9 → 2.0 THE CATACOMBS. The deliverables specify proposed behavior and asset targets for subsequent passes.

## Midnight Festival · Keep a place lit

At midnight Cinder Court rehanges the shutters from shops that never reopened. Their painted undersides make a communal table across the wall. One place setting is blank. The Archivist offers the cat a way to light the missing place by walking a pair of lantern posts while the street is still alive with enemies.

**Enter by moving.** The inherited start position is (1340, 1340) in Cinder Court, the only initially open district. Use a pure unsigned hash of the inherited city seed with a festival salt. A roll of one in eight qualifies the run. Within qualifying seeds a second independent hash selects a mirrored pair of posts and which post has a Cinder Draft. No consumption of combat, fishing, offer or terrain RNG. Pin one qualifying seed in the candidate index as a clearly named Festival Run link using the existing seed parameter and ordinary Start. Unseeded runs keep the rare roll. Pass 04 must record the hash, salt, featured seed and deterministic layout fixtures.

**Read the street.** Stage the grounded Archivist and painted shutter mural near spawn, with two clear walking routes out from the starting view. Nominal layout offsets from spawn are (±112, −64) for the posts and (0, −105) for the mural, mirrored by the seed. Validate against blockers, fishing sockets and routes from spawn, using the inherited clearance model with player radius plus 12 world units. Check a route around each hazard and leave the central approach traversable. Check a fixed ordered set of alternate offsets if needed. Suppress the event and log the reason if the pair cannot fit. Thumbnails and captions cannot stand in for the world-space reveal.

| State | Active game time | Entry, work and exit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dormant | 0–4 s | Normal start, validate layout. Ineligible seeds and stress-start modes stay ordinary. |
| Lighting | 4–8 s | Enter automatically in Cinder Court. Warm windows ignite in sequence, bunting lanterns sway, silhouettes toast and the Archivist opens a folding ledger. Show one short festival status and begin the stem after inherited audio unlock. |
| Listening | 8–44 s | Walk inside either post's 36-unit circle for 0.75 continuous seconds. Leave the circle and partial dwell resets. Each post stamps once. HUD shows 0/2 through 2/2 and remaining time. Route order is free. Stamp two settles immediately. |
| Settled | On second stamp | Latch success before granting two loose fish units once via the ordinary drop path. Place the reward at a checked clear point by the second post. Fish can be picked up, eaten or stolen under existing rules. The final mural panel lights and the Archivist closes the book. |
| Fading | 3 s starting at success, timeout or district departure | Disable event progress, new draft effects and reward attempts. Clear transient gameplay tags immediately. Fade local lights and audio, then retire. |
| Retired | Rest of run | No repeated festival, reward or revisits. Restart constructs fresh run state. |

Timeout at 44 s wins over any stamp at the deadline. Leaving Cinder Court after lighting begins settles as abandonment. Losing a life after lighting begins cancels the event before revival. Game over, victory and restart clean up immediately. Pause, cards and hidden tabs suspend active time, clear queued audio and silence the stem, then resume from the same state without backfilling sound or movement. The event follows active simulation time across the inherited 13-second nights.

**The choice.** The cool post is the easier first route. Going for the draft early leaves time for a second approach. Walking away preserves positioning and any committed fishing opportunity. The seeded draft paints a striped 52-unit circle beside one post with a safe approach outside the circle. Every six seconds during Listening, give a 1-second directional warning, then briefly hurry at most two eligible ordinary ground enemies already crossing it by 15% for at most 1.25 s, once per enemy per gust. Keep their heading, collision, ranges and damage. Exclude elite, boss, city gate/guardian, summoned, rallied and mutant actors. At the festival's first safe ordinary director admission during Listening, prefer a base shambler over the otherwise eligible ordinary ground variant at equal or lower cost, using the usual spawn socket, threat and accounting. Skip the preference if the director declines or the compatible candidate never occurs. No bonus spawn. The draft may be empty. Its warning reads with mute on. An active Scout may point out the least recently visited unstamped post once through the inherited speech channel after a successful mark. It still performs its normal combat work.

**One scene.** At lighting the Archivist unfolds a book wide enough to hide behind. “They took the chairs. I kept the places.” At the first stamp, the painted table gains a saucer. At success, one little silhouette sets another beside it. “Leave that one empty. She likes to arrive late.” On departure or timeout the Archivist folds the final blank page inward, with no extra dialogue. No modal camera or quest panel. Give the two lines one shared low-priority caption lane and allow an urgent warning to replace either.

The Archivist is a nonrecruitable NPC for 1.69. Its event sensing is the automatic read and visible glyph when the player nears a post during Listening. It has no collision, combat, healing, roster entry, offer weight, feeding or progression. The seventeenth recruit is deferred because offer generation, food routing and four-slot fusion accounting would all need new regression coverage for a role whose event function works locally. Give the NPC an original low tortoiseshell body, oversized cream forepaws, a split ear and a long tail looped through the ledger's brass handle. Its silhouette changes when it leans onto the open pages and when it pulls them shut. Preserve the Bard's identity.

## Automatic squad resonance

Available throughout ordinary runs, so a mature squad can use it after the festival has ended. Two triggers share a single run-wide 10-second cooldown from emission, with at most two emissions per inherited night. The cooldown crosses night boundaries. Evaluate completed simulation actions once by serial, in update order. If two qualify in the same tick, Mecha comes first, then stable squad order. Missing a trigger never queues a delayed pulse.

- **Mecha Crew + Scout.** A fired Mecha volley during its actual fire stage can resonate if a live Scout within 140 units marked that target within the last two active seconds. Check live squad membership at emission. Boarding and repair provide no trigger.
- **Courier + Scout.** A completed Courier delivery to a distinct live Scout within 140 units can resonate after a successful existing food transaction. Self-delivery, ordinary foraging and failed claims provide no trigger.

Both participants and hero must be in the same open district and hero within 180 units of the source. The wave marks up to three nearest eligible ordinary ground foes within 120 units and unobstructed by terrain. Slow movement by 20% for 1.25 s, with no damage, interrupt, healing, invulnerability or resource grant. Skip drafted foes and all draft-ineligible classes. Apply the stronger of this and existing slows, with separate expiry. If no eligible target is present, spend neither cooldown nor emission. One 450 ms radial drawing and one shared bus cue confirm a successful proc. Strip resonance slow on district change or life loss and clear audiovisual transients on pause. Keep the remaining cooldown after either. Positioning near squad and enemies can earn a short escape window. The solo Archivist glyph uses a different shape and sound and has zero combat effect. The inherited Level 3 Engineer + Level 3 Shooter fusion still occupies one slot. Both triggers are synergy rules for existing identities.

## First-minute evidence and mobile staging

On the featured seed, ordinary Start shows illuminated shutters, animated Archivist and festival sound by active second 8, and lets a solo player choose a post immediately. By second 44 the posts resolve, with time before minute one to see the street return to normal. For the aura portion of the same first-minute acceptance, use a separately labeled QA fixture on the featured seed with legal Scout/Courier or Mecha/Scout membership and live combat. Trigger via actual work ticks and capture the effect on enemies and shared audio. A fresh run starts solo and the normal first-minute offer and levelling schedule cannot guarantee either pair. The candidate index must identify which capture uses fixture setup.

Keep the upper safe-area status to one line, with a post count, numerical seconds and a segmented bar. In portrait and landscape hold status and captions outside the thumb origin, pause, fish and life HUD. Use 14 CSS px minimum, 4.5:1 text contrast, no pointer events and a single short caption of at most two lines. Keep attack telegraphs on top of festival lights. Avoid full-screen tint, shake and flashing. No active controls are added. Preserve the inherited offscreen arrow's existing targets and priority. Offscreen festival prompts are text only.
