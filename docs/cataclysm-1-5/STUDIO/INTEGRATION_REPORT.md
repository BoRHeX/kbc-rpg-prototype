## Implemented source

- CAT-15-01: `game.js` fishingInit/fishingTick add eight seed-positioned spots in existing district street corridors. Dry radius-52 aprons, solid water rectangles and existing walls share collision authority. Each spot is accessible from its district plaza; gates still control access between districts. One second stationary dwell commits a cast. Movement input, displacement, damage, leaving, pause/end cancel; automatic reel resolves after 4–6 seconds minus rod benefit. Three committed attempts per spot, including cancellations; four-second post-catch rest. No manual fishing/combat control. Original tail gun continues firing.
- CAT-15-02: five additional eligible random cards use inherited choice/reroll dispatch: rod/line ranks capped at two; worm six charges, glowing fishbone four, royal catnip two. Cards equip bait; exhausted bait becomes unlimited plain lure. At most one fishing card per offer. Catnip requires three caught fish units. Rod shortens resolution; line shifts 2% ambush per rank to ordinary fish. Original card pool remains.
- CAT-15-03: fishingOdds uses the concept's six-column weight tables. Outcomes are normal fish, legendary Lantern Koi, treasure/reroll, jackpot cache, mutant or three-enemy ambush. Risk transfers 4% ordinary weight into ambush every eight stationary seconds, capped at three tiers. Dedicated integer RNG combines city seed, cryptographic run nonce, spot and monotonic committed ordinal. Outcome is reserved before bait debit; cancellation cannot reroll that ordinal and releases a committed hazard. Cache weight becomes normal after first cache. Each run has only 24 possible attempts. Award IDs and resolved IDs prevent duplicate credits.
- Actual mutant: inherited damageable enemy actor with genuine mutant illustration; 24 HP, one-damage contact, arrival grace, repeating one-second aimed warning, 0.45-second 360-unit/s collision-backed lunge, then recovery. Tail/crew attacks can kill it; reward six fish and 500 JACKPOT. Ambush uses ordinary combat actors, not particles. Hazard population limited to six; no terrain-ignoring mutant traits.
- CAT-15-04: all catch units enter addFish and COMPANY.ledger.spawned. `fishing.caught` is provenance, not spendable currency or a second ledger credit. Physical pickup/eating remains inherited and atomic; companions gain their own food without player fish/XP duplication. Courier feeds the lowest-energy recipient; Angler retains reserved autonomous catches. Ordinary drops reduced from configured 65% to 9%; four district caches give four physical fish each. Boss rewards and guardian six-fish rewards provide alternatives. Player kill XP and solo perks remain. Legacy final 999-fish transaction now also credits the ledger. Fishing never gates campaign progression.
- CAT-15-05: separate JACKPOT, capped at 10,000: legendary +250, cache +1000 once, mutant defeat +500, each guardian provocation +100/defeat +500, low-HP proximity fishing +100 once per spot. HUD and death/victory copy distinguish JACKPOT from inherited run score and Night-100 completion bonus. No score multiplier added.
- Spawn perimeter: isTerrainClear rejects exterior coordinates; nearestSafeSpace clamps search origins inside physical city limits. safeSpawn accepts open-district candidates and has collision-clear reachable fallback sockets. Ordinary and catch spawns stay inside city; this does not redesign inherited enemy navigation.
- Version: actual `index.html` boot expectation, visible title/version and game BUILD are 1.5. Four seeded districts, beacon gates/relief, guardians, independent arrow, paused map, nine lives, sixteen roles, four slots, illustrated Mecha and hero/tail remain. ## Not implemented / deviations

The concept is broader than this integration. No paused-map fishing discovery icons or odds panel, manual inventory bait switching, clean independent rod rig, finite inventory pack stacking, special water polygon/bridge topology, or eight independently painted locations. Spots use small collision rectangles/patches in existing streets. No guarantee of a fishing-family offer weight ceiling beyond one per offer; exact 20% family weighting is not implemented. Fishbone eligibility is broader than the proposed resolved-catch gate. Risk time persists rather than resetting after 20 seconds outside. Pause discards the active attempt and does not preserve its pending hazard; no resume/save-run support. A seeded city repeats layout but run nonces intentionally change catches; browser storage tamper resistance is not claimed. Full multi-seed flood-fill/navigation proof and statistical campaign economy balance are not complete. Danger suppression at the hazard cap yields no replacement reward. Mutant health is fixed rather than night-scaled, and warning art has not been visually accepted. No secondary fusions were added. Inherited 1.4 art debt and difficulty concerns remain.

CAT-15-01 through 06 are implemented with the stated deviations; CAT-15-07 is PARTIAL (deterministic checks, browser blocked). This report supersedes copied predecessor integration claims only for this 1.5 source; it does not claim all original concept deliverables complete.
