## Play promise and seeded places — CAT-15-01

These are compact additions to existing streets, not four replacement ground squares or new districts. Guarantee a reachable starting sewer spot 300–700 route units from spawn. Later spots become reachable through inherited gates.

For each socket store stable ID, district, type, water polygon, dry apron, cast anchor, line endpoint, exit route, enemy entry sockets and discovery flag. Try up to 32 deterministic candidate placements per spot. Validate bounds, inflated collision, unlocked component accessibility, 120-unit dry approach/escape width, gate/beacon/guardian-plaza clearance ≥180, and water exclusion from traversal. Reject isolated dry islands and docks with a single body-blockable exit; retain a validated template fallback per district, never an unchecked coordinate. ## Movement and timing contract — CAT-15-01/06

| State | Entry / timing | Exit and effects |
|---|---|---|
| Idle | Player center enters radius-48 dry apron; no hostile within 120 | After actual speed <8 units/s and no movement input for 0.25s, begin windup |
| Windup | 0.80s planted anticipation | Movement input above inherited deadzone OR displacement >6 units resets immediately; no bait spent |
| Cast | 0.35s painted rod release | At first cast frame commit attempt ID, outcome reservation and one finite bait charge atomically |
| Wait | Seeded 2.5–5.0s, campaign continues | Bite is automatic; no tapping window; ambient risk tell at least 0.8s before any hazard |
| Bite / reel | 0.65s automatic reel | Resolve once at end, credit catch or release hazard; no reward before this point |
| Recover | 0.45s acting; same spot rest 4s | Movement always allowed; can exit without waiting for animation |
| Cancel | Movement input above deadzone, apron exit radius 56, displacement >6, damage, death, end or restart | Cancel before the same-frame completion check; logical cancellation ≤1 simulation tick (target ≤50ms), reel visual/audio tail ≤0.25s; no root, slow or invulnerability |

After commitment, cancellation consumes bait and attempt, grants no catch/jackpot, and cannot reroll that attempt. A precommitted hazard still emits a warned entry near the spot after cancellation; leaving remains safe because no enemy tracks a teleport onto the player. If no valid entry exists, suppress hazard and record it without granting reward. Pause freezes all timers, never commits or completes a catch. Unpause requires fresh stationary dwell. Overlapping spots choose nearest then stable ID; only one active attempt. Tail aiming/firing continues independently; fishing art must not add a second gun or hide the tail. Rod rank reduces reel by 0.1s/rank (minimum 0.45s), not movement cancellation speed.
