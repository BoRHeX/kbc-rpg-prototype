# CATACLYSM 1.0 — THE CATASTROPHE
## Definitive major-rebuild work order · 2026-11-11 · Creator-authorized

**Goal:** A genuinely substantial improvement of the existing Cataclysm 1.0 playtest: richer audiovisual identity, more varied combat, substantial world, readable UI, strategic progression, and a finite 100-night campaign. This is **not** permission for a cosmetic update, version rename, or a two-feature minimum. All seven feature families below are mandatory before marking the major build done.

**Baseline:** `docs/cataclysm-1-0-preview/` is the verified 1.0 playtest, developed from the latest illustrated 0.9. Its source is one ~133 KB, ~2,060-line `game.js`; the old generalist `MOBILE_CODER_PROMPT.md` only allows four game files. **Do not feed this work order into the old monolithic release pipeline.** That system is for smaller feature changes and would block modular ownership. **Do not modify `docs/cataclysm/` (stable 0.9), `docs/preview/`, other games, Flame Game Studio, existing releases, secrets, review recordings or the studio dashboard.** Build the candidate in `docs/cataclysm-1-0-major/` under a separate branch until accepted. The current playable 1.0 preview must stay available.

### Creator requirements (no voting any item away)

**R1 — Music and sound.** Compose **two NEW distinct original songs/melodic arrangements** in addition to existing adaptive survival/boss music, with meaningful motifs, sections, transitions and event-based layering (not just two renamed patterns). Expand the set of distinct SFX with cues for damage, ultimates, pickups, enemy cast, boss telegraph, elite hunt, reroll, victory and failure. Browser user-gesture audio, mute, volume, pause, no run-away audio nodes, and sound/game event synchronization are mandatory. No copyrighted third-party audio.

**R2 — Animated articulated characters.** All 9 normal enemies + 9 bosses + 6 elites + 3 super-elites require an **actual 6-frame animation** each: head, torso, legs, arms, tail independently composed/posed (27 archetypes × 6 = 162 frames minimum). Eight squad members require **12 frames each** (=96). The main white cat requires **24 frames per named state** for at least idle, locomotion, damage, feral, adrenaline (=120); feral and adrenaline must be visually unique, not just colored rings. Minimum tested clips: 378 distinct frame/state selections. Build a layered rig / sprite-atlas architecture rather than 378 unrelated hardcoded drawings. Hit/attack cues, damage flash and reduced-motion behavior remain. Count tests are necessary but not sufficient: animation must actually read in-game.

**R3 — Richer larger map.** Expand horizontal exploration substantially over the current 2,400-unit cylinder while retaining stable downward chunk streaming and biome transitions. Add visibly larger navigable buildings, dumpsters and parked cars (with useful cover, collisions and enemy route planning); recognizable biome-specific variations. Maintain offscreen spawning, legible encounters, navigability and bounded chunk memory. No walls spawning on actors or unescapable scenery; keep predictable camera and touch behavior.

**R4 — UI and fully automatic abilities.** Replace text-button-heavy gameplay chrome with readable icons and indicators: ability triggering, active timers, cooldown recovery, buffs, debuffs, squad status, score, night, boss telegraph, reroll currency. Automatically activate all companion abilities and other gameplay abilities when their cooldown finishes and they are valid to fire; no requirement for manual taps to use them. Target-aware abilities must wait for meaningful targets rather than waste charges; healing must not waste charge at full health. Separate tactical controls (if retained) from automated combat abilities; preserve mute, pause and accessibility. All controls must fit on a Samsung landscape screen and smaller tested viewports.

**R5 — More upgrades and boss/elite rerolls.** Expand viable offensive, movement, defensive, companion and equipment branches with meaningful synergies, choices and tradeoffs; preserve existing 3-choice roguelite offers, freeze-on-choice, eight permanent companion classes with four unique active maximum, no unowned companion offers after four. Bosses and elites drop reroll tokens as a bounded resource; reroll all three offers without duplicate/stale offers, infinite loops, softlocks, or loss of XP/upgrade state. Display reroll inventory and explain where tokens come from. Super-elites should be a useful, stronger source, not a farmable exploit.

**R6 — Enemy roster.** Exactly 9 normal enemy archetypes (3 melee, 3 ranged, 3 caster); casters must actively maintain distance and reposition, avoid walls, telegraph cast and be interruptible/counterable. Also 9 distinct boss archetypes, 6 elite archetypes and 3 super-elite archetypes, with visibly and mechanically distinct attack patterns, telegraphs and counters; no mere HP/color/name clones. Modular trait/mutation/fusion may modify an archetype but may **not** inflate the archetype count. Distinguish persistent-type IDs and instance IDs. Preserve offscreen safe spawning and a bounded threat director; no forced hits at spawn.

**R7 — Finite 100-night campaign.** Night duration **13 seconds** simulation time. Night 1 starts at t=0; night 100 occupies t=1287–1300s. Boss events every ninth night (9,18,...,99) = 11 scheduled boss encounters at 117s intervals, drawing from 9 unique boss types with two advanced rematches. Elite wave events every sixth night (6,12,...,96) = 16 scheduled 78s intervals. Overlap on nights 18,36,54,72,90: encounter scheduling must honor both and throttle threat, never silently skip or double-charge a budget. Distinguish six normal elite archetypes and three super-elite archetypes.

**Working Night-100 victory interpretation:** by the end of Night 100, player has defeated at least 9 bosses, 9 elite enemies, and 9 super-elite enemies **over the run**, and cleared the final wave while alive. On verified victory set score to exactly **999,999,999**, show a true Victory screen, freeze simulation, persist the result. Do not grant that score on survival alone, partial kills or debug instrumentation. Make scripted kill opportunities actually attainable within ~21m40s; allow a short bounded resolution interval only if documented. See `DECISIONS.md`: the exact interpretation of the creator's “night 100 9 bosses 9 elites and 9 super+elites wave” remains a Product Owner question, not a reason to omit the entire campaign. Implement the schedule and count ledger as independent interfaces that can be adjusted without replacing combat logic.

### Major design principles
1. **Pacing is gameplay.** Waves, recovery windows, exploration and combo windows should be perceptible and enjoyable to play/watch. Do not use bulk spawns to fake difficulty.
2. **Meaningful uniqueness.** Every new enemy uses a behavior profile and attack definition; every visual clip selects distinct articulated poses. Any nominal-only implementation is a failure.
3. **Immutable approved baseline.** Extract functions or adapt them into modules while preserving existing 1.0 controls/fusion/shadow/companion mechanics, and all surviving test contracts.
4. **One integration owner.** Specialists edit their own modules and tests. The Integrator alone updates shared imports, top-level game loop and binding contracts.
5. **Traceability.** Every requirement R1–R7 must identify code, executable test, browser evidence and reviewer approval. No agent can reduce scope or mark its own work as independent QA.
6. **Mobile-first performance.** Frame timing, particle/actor caps, audio voice limits, low-memory devices, touch input, reduced-motion and accessibility are acceptance criteria, not polish.
7. **Video-first feedback loop.** Keep development candidates isolated, retain a deterministic seed for demos, record source/version/QA evidence. User review makes the next revision, not autonomous guessing about enjoyment.

### Out of scope
Publishing to app stores, monetization, multiplayer, social features, unlicensed audio/art, any edits to Flame Game Studio, and replacing current public 0.9 or 1.0 playtest before QA.

**Definition of done:** complete R1–R7, independent deterministic gameplay simulation through Night 100, eight touch/viewport browser checks, visual/audio review evidence, explicit reviewer acceptance, separately deployed and link-verified major build, and a rollback source ref. An API call completing, a Codex 'success' result, or an HTML page rendering does **not** mean the game is finished.
