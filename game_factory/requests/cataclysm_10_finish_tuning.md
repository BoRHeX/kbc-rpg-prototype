Enforce a density/active-role limit (e.g., no more than roughly a third of normal mobs as ranged/casters) and sensible initial encounter timing. A ghost currently auto-silences at <=220 range for 2s every 8s even without a visible projectile: make this a properly telegraphed, more expensive and *less frequent* counterable ability, ideally checking actual range/line-of-sight and shared cooldown when several ghosts are nearby. Do NOT delete enemy archetypes or nullify ranged danger; players should be able to dodge and clear with a solo main cat. Avoid trivializing the game; retain late escalation and meaningful bosses. Make sure both `game.js` and `director.js` costs agree and all checks remain coherent.
