## Musical creative direction
Not a few bleep oscillator notes! Make an ORIGINAL, addictive arcade **hard-rock / drum-and-bass hybrid** battle soundtrack. A **boss entering** should trigger an obvious, intentional **DUBSTEP DROP**; boss defeat restores the driving normal score, with a satisfying musical resolution. No samples of existing copyrighted recordings, no borrowed melody or imitation of specific non-public-domain music/artists. Everything must be procedurally synthesized via existing Web Audio API (AudioContext), no fetching external music, sound files, mp3, CDNs, dependencies or autoplay-policy violations. Efficient enough for Samsung mobile, single audio context, bounded voice/polyphony, reusable synthesized buffers where sensible, compressed/limited master bus to prevent clipping, no recurring setInterval/worker timers.

### Core SURVIVAL soundtrack
- Strong rhythm: roughly 170–176 BPM **drum & bass drive** with synthesized punchy kick, cracking/snappy snare on backbeats, rhythmic closed/open hi-hats, ghost hits and occasional short fills. Blend with an assertive **hard rock** character: distorted/chugging low guitar-like power-chord riff and sub/overdrive bass line with rhythmic rests, some higher hook or chord changes. Musical phrases (4–8 bars), not random unrelated frequencies. The user should recognize it as energetic music rather than generic 1980s game beeps.
- Syncopation/groove and deliberate balance between rhythm, bass and melody; don't swamp action SFX. At minimum 3 distinctive percussive timbres and 2 musical voices. Groove should feel consistent across player survival, levels and encounters (optionally increase intensity with survival time without overwhelming).
- Create original progression and rhythms from explicit scale/key choices. Dynamic instruments using oscillators, waveshaping/filter/envelopes, reusable noise buffers (once, not per-note allocations), filter sweeps when appropriate. Audio gains low enough for headphones.

### BOSS soundtrack / dubstep transition
- When the Alley King is ALIVE (existing `game.enemies.some(e=>e.variant==='boss')`), switch to a radically distinct, powerful ~138–145 BPM HALF-TIME dubstep groove: heavy kick/snare, quantized wobble/growl mid-bass with time-varying resonant lowpass/filter or LFO, sub drops, sparing stabs and controlled rhythmic breaks. Add a short anticipation/hit at the moment of boss entrance so the drop is felt even with gameplay audio.
- Transition quickly (<~1 second) when boss appears; never run BOTH score modes at full volume; switch back to normal survival when boss is defeated, with a brief resolving impact, no stuck boss music. Avoid infinitely queued old notes when mode changes: bounded scheduling lookahead (~0.1–0.15 s), cancel/duck old music with master gain and clean patterns/voices. Boss detection must not rely only on wall clock if paused; pause/upgrades/death silence music smoothly.
- After death, restart or replay, there must be no double percussion tracks, leaked nodes, or stale song state.

### Game SOUND EFFECTS (not just music)
- Upgrade sound effects to recognizable, short and satisfying actions: sharp firearm report and varied impact for player/companions, dry crunchy hit/critical hit, bright fish XP pickup, strong but not grating level-up cue, healing tone, companion shooter volley/tank guard/healer pulse, powerup can pickup, danger warning and boss intro/death.
- Preserve existing `sound(freq,len,type,volume)` call contract so no runtime failures. New `playSfx(kind,...)` or equivalent allowed; integrate calls at existing event sites without changing the mechanical results/timing. Not every single shot needs a big bass event; enforce rate limiting/polyphony for repeated gunfire and crowded scenes. Musical score must remain audible when the game gets busy.
- Maintain mute button semantics for ALL sounds (background plus effects), respect browser requires first Play/touch gesture, support running/paused/upgrade/dead/restart, hidden-tab auto-pause and no zombie sounds afterwards. Do not accidentally create AudioContext on page load. When sound is off, no new voices.
