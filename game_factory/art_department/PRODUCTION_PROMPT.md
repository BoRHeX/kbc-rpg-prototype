### What the art needs to do

The creator likes the existing **upper-body dual-pistol aiming**; preserve its identity, tracking and weapon legibility. The failure is the **lower half**: the legs look bolted in place even though many sprite files pass automated tests. Build a true motion system with identifiable thigh, knee, calf, paw/foot and hip motion. Across the gait, each foot must alternately plant, support weight, push off, lift, pass and land; the knee should bend and extend. Make the footfalls visibly different at **48px gameplay size**. The tail, scarf, breathing chest, blinking face and arm recoil should show delayed response, but these details cannot substitute for leg movement. Avoid full-body vertical bobbing, mirrored two-pose loops, gradient color flicker, clipping artifacts or double silhouettes disguised as rich animation. Preserve 8-way aim independently of locomotion and retain a single complete hero sprite draw per frame. Original art or a purpose-built rig from a single painted identity is acceptable; **you choose the creative method, not the old generator's tiny ±2px leg swing**.

States: `idle` breath and weight-shift; `walk` grounded distinct slow footfall; `run` full-stride; `sprint` more powerful running; `overdrive` visibly extreme but readable movement; `shield-idle` and `shield-run` protective stance *with actual moving legs*; `hit` meaningful recoil and collapse/recovery; `recover` return to posture; `attack` anticipation and independent pistol recoil; `run-fire` gunfire without freezing the gait; `feral` and `adrenaline` expressive animated accelerated runs. Keep the user-facing version **Cataclysm 1.0**.

For integration, add a **motion profile** parameter to `CATA_ART.draw` as a final optional argument (existing callers unchanged), e.g. `{speedRatio,shielded,previewState}`. In normal gameplay `game.js` must pass current measured player motion speed, shield status and active buffs so the atlas actually switches between idle/walk/run/sprint/overdrive/shielded/hit. For independent previews, a `previewState` allows sampling a specific state from the same canonical pack. Preserve automatic enemy-facing barrel aim. Explicitly support test speed ratios: 0→idle; 0.43→walk; 1→run; 1.48→sprint; 2.2 overdrive→overdrive; 0 with shield→shield-idle; 1 with shield→shield-run; moving feral→feral; moving adrenaline→adrenaline; hit→hit. You may improve animation timing and thresholds provided these recognizable scenarios remain correct. No new player controls.

The independent image-motion gate checks anatomical foot motion **relative to the torso**, rejecting static legs even if the gun arms move or the entire sprite bobs. The real-game Chrome adapter injects temporary QA (not deployed) and proves each actual gameplay condition draws precisely ONE image from the newly authored family, as well as reviewing contact sheets of every state and all 24 running frames. The independent visual art director scores real running-game imagery, pose quality and leg readability at native mobile size, not just mathematically different files.

**Don't deliver a technical success with immobile legs.** If time is limited, prioritize convincing walk, run, sprint, overdrive and shielded footwork before minor accessories. The entire job is bounded by the studio pipeline and accepted only when those visual gates succeed.
