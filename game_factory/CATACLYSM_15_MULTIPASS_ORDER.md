## Seven authoritative 1.5 feature IDs (in master ledger)
- **CAT-15-01:** Discover **actual fishing spots** in the seeded city (waterfront, canal, sewer pond) with game-world marker/ripple. Enter, stand for a brief casting windup and automatically cast. Moving out of the spot **cancels/reels in**, risk can summon enemies, and the player can always ignore fishing. Natural gameplay; no new manual control.
- **CAT-15-02:** Equip and upgrade rod, line, and bait through eligible random roguelite cards without erasing inherited card variety/rerolls. Start with modest base kit; include worm, glowing fishbone and rare royal catnip and meaningful catch-table effects.
- **CAT-15-03:** Weighted seeded catches with understandable odds and danger: normal fish, rare fish, treasure, jackpot cache, **giant mutant fish** or ambush. Fishing isn't a guaranteed riskless income machine. Increase risk with time and bait, and avoid repeatable fixed-seed jackpots and softlock traps.
- **CAT-15-04:** Companions eat their own fish and forage independently, already true in 1.4. Fishing joins the atomic fish ledger without duplication. Ordinary drops reduced, cash/fish caches and boss rewards provide alternatives; player XP and solo viability preserved. Angler's special catches and Courier's feeding gain tangible importance.
- **CAT-15-05:** Distinct **JACKPOT** score counter for rare catches and daring near-death moments and guardian provocation/defeats. No runaway multiplication: historical Night-100 999,999,999 completion score remains but must not make a catch jackpot illegible or exploitable. End screen distinguishes fishing jackpot from legacy run score.
- **CAT-15-06:** Actual illustration-based fishing spot, water/line/cast/reel animations, original fish/treasure/mutant sprites, fitting original audio cues and mobile HUD. Drawn PNG/rig animation primary; Canvas circles/lines are suitable for line physics and VFX, not substitute character illustration.
- **CAT-15-07:** Deterministic tests of fish debit/credit, caught-vs-eaten accounting, bait probability shifts, jackpot caps, cast cancellation, ambush spawns, valid spot geometry and solo/squad progression. Browser start/nonblank in portrait and landscape and run-level sanity checks. Failing tests do NOT discard code; label candidate.

## Requested secondary changes from actual 1.4 playthrough
- **Wall/spawner concern:** prevent ordinary enemies and catch ambushes from spawning outside reachable district boundaries. Search for spawns beyond wall; handle solid collision, reachable spawn sockets and readable enemy entry, but do not make 1.5 map redesign replace fishing work.
- **Art:** 1.4 used illustrated sprites to great effect but visibly stretched four-square ground tiles and a handful of guardian assets. Draw actual fishing locations integrated with the city rather than new tiled squares disguised as new worlds. Keep recognizable artistic style and keep texture alignment/mobile performance.
- **Challenge:** Night-100 clear with nine lives again suggests experienced player power can overwhelm existing enemies. Fishing should introduce meaningful choices/risk and reduced fish surplus, not merely buff an already invincible cat. Keep diverse solo and companion play viable.
- **Companion fusion documentation:** pause guide should accurately list currently implemented pair (Engineer+Shooter) and reserve future combinations as unknown/not unlocked, not pretend other fusions exist.
